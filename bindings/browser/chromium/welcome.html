Before you start

Canoa lets an agent you have paired use this browser. That is useful, and it has risks you should know first.

What an agent can do with a tab you give it

  • Read it. Its text and what can be clicked in it, including anything you are signed in to there.
  • Act in it, if you allow that too. Click and type as you, on that site.
  • Be talked into things. A page can contain text meant to steer an agent. Share only what you would show the agent yourself, and be careful with banking, mail and anything private.

What Canoa does about it

  • Nothing is shared until you choose it, one tab at a time. Pairing an agent gives it no tab.
  • What you allow belongs to one tab and one site, and ends when the tab closes or goes to another page.
  • Acting on a page, as against reading it, is a separate question. The browser's own prompt comes first for a site it has not been given.
  • Take any of it back under Canoa browser permissions.
